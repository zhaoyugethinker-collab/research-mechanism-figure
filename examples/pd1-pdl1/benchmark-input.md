## Verified minimal summary

PD-1 is an inhibitory receptor expressed on activated T cells and other immune cells. PD-L1 can be expressed by tumor cells and other cells. Engagement of PD-1 by PD-L1 can deliver an inhibitory co-signal that reduces T-cell effector activity and weakens antitumor responses, thereby facilitating immune escape in some contexts. This is not the only tumor-evasion mechanism and does not imply universal PD-L1 expression or treatment response.
