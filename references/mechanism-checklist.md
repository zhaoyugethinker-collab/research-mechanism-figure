# Mechanism figure checklist

## Before drawing

- [ ] Figure purpose, audience, biological context, and scope are clear.
- [ ] Each claim maps to a paper, cited note, confirmed relationship, or clearly labeled hypothesis.
- [ ] Cells and molecules are assigned to the correct cell, compartment, or side of a membrane.
- [ ] Binding, activation, inhibition, translocation, expression, and outcome are not conflated.
- [ ] Arrow/line starts, endpoints, and meanings are written down.
- [ ] Uncertain, conflicting, and context-specific relationships are visible in the review table.
- [ ] A user or named human reviewer confirms the relationship table before image generation.

## Inspect the generated image

- [ ] No required element or confirmed edge is missing.
- [ ] No unsupported molecule, cell, pathway, treatment, or causal link was added.
- [ ] Arrow direction and inhibitory/activating symbols match the confirmed table.
- [ ] Labels are spelled correctly and attached to the right structures.
- [ ] The image is readable at its intended display size and is not visually cluttered.
- [ ] Conceptual illustrations are labeled as conceptual and not experimental data.
- [ ] No published figure was copied or traced.

## Inspect editable output

- [ ] Text can be edited separately.
- [ ] Cell/node shapes can be moved separately.
- [ ] Arrows/connectors can be selected separately.
- [ ] The slide is not just a single raster image.
- [ ] The author records whether each check was performed in the target presentation app or inferred only from file structure.
